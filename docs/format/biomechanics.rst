Biomechanical Data
==================

SWIM is designed to accommodate multiple types of swimming
biomechanical measurements.


Kinematics
----------

Kinematic data may include measurements such as:

* Position
* Velocity
* Acceleration
* Joint trajectories
* Body-segment measurements


Inertial measurement units
---------------------------

IMU data may include:

* Accelerometer measurements
* Gyroscope measurements
* Magnetometer measurements
* Sensor metadata
* Sampling information


Force measurements
------------------

Force-related data can be stored together with the metadata required
to interpret the measurements.


Stroke metrics
--------------

Stroke-related measurements may include:

* Stroke rate
* Stroke length
* Stroke count
* Stroke phase information
* Other derived performance metrics


Video synchronization
---------------------

SWIM can associate biomechanical measurements with video data through
synchronization metadata.

The actual video files do not necessarily need to be embedded in the
SWIM file.
